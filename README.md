### 💡 About Me

> **Building AI systems that solve real business problems, not just impressive demos.**

I'm an **AI Systems Engineer** with **5+ years of experience** designing, building, and deploying production-grade AI solutions that help organizations automate complex workflows, unlock business insights, and create intelligent products at scale.

Throughout my career, I've worked across the entire AI lifecycle — from solution architecture and foundational research to deployment, optimization, and scaling. I enjoy turning ambitious ideas into reliable, resilient AI systems that deliver measurable business value.

- 🚀 **Proven Track Record**: Delivered **20+ AI projects** across startups and international teams, building platforms that improved operational efficiency by up to **70%**.
- 🛠️ **Full-Stack AI Domain**: Developed production-ready products spanning **Generative AI**, **Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Orchestration**, **Computer Vision**, **NLP**, **Intelligent Analytics**, and **Enterprise Automation**.
- 🔬 **Frontier Research**: Fine-tuning compact language models using DeepSeek-R1's **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** to elicit autonomous `<think>` chain-of-thought deliberation for mathematical problem solving ([Hugging Face Model](https://huggingface.co/Rauhan/llama-3.2-3B-GRPO-GSM325)).
- 🎯 **Engineering Philosophy**: Beyond writing code, I'm passionate about designing robust AI architectures, solving challenging engineering bottlenecks, and building products people genuinely rely on. I believe the future of AI belongs not only to powerful models, but to **reliable, scalable systems that create lasting impact.**
